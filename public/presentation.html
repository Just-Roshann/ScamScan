PS-008: AI-Powered Scam & Phishing Detection System

🎯 1. What is ScamScan? (The 10-Second Pitch)

"ScamScan is like a digital security guard for your family. You paste any weird message, email, website link, or upload a WhatsApp screenshot — and within 0.3 seconds, it gives you a simple 0 to 100 Risk Score, explains exactly why it’s dangerous in plain English, highlights the fake parts, and gives you official 1-click Indian cyber helplines (1930 & Chakshu)."

The Problem: Over ₹1,750 Crores was lost to digital fraud in India in early 2024 alone. Ordinary citizens are bombarded with fake SBI KYC messages, electricity cutoff threats, digital arrest scams, and part-time job lures. Existing antivirus tools only block known malware files — they are totally blind to social engineering, fake urgency, and Hinglish deception.

💻 3. Tech Stack & Implementation Details

Layer Technology What It Does in Simple Words
Frontend React + Vite + TailwindCSS Sleek, responsive dark/light UI. Smooth animations with Framer Motion, accessible gauge charts, and instant tabs.
Screenshot OCR Tesseract.js (Client-side) Reads text from WhatsApp/SMS screenshots right inside the browser. Zero image upload delay, 100% private.
Backend API Python 3.11 + FastAPI High-speed asynchronous REST API. Validates schemas with Pydantic and returns full analysis in under 300ms.
Fast AI Engine Groq Cloud (LPU Inference) Executes ultra-low latency LLMs (GPT-OSS / Llama) in ~80ms. Understands deception, tone, and Hinglish.
Rule Engine Custom Python Heuristics Brand spoofing (Levenshtein distance), shady TLD check (.xyz, .top), regex for OTP/UPI, and 12 scam dictionaries.
Website Inspector httpx + BeautifulSoup Safely fetches live websites without executing malicious JavaScript. Detects hidden password fields and domain spoofing.

🏆 4. Why ScamScan Wins (Our USPs)

1. True Hybrid Architecture

Pure AI hallucinates; pure rules miss context. We fuse both. Transparent rule evidence + contextual AI reasoning.

2. Zero-Downtime Resilience

If Groq AI has network lag or rate limits, the app never crashes. The Rule Engine steps in with full protection.

3. Anti-Hallucination Filter

Every reason produced by the AI is strictly verified against the actual text. The AI cannot make up facts.

4. Tailored for Indian Frauds

Detects KYC expiration, Part-Time YouTube tasks, Digital Arrest CBI threats, Electricity disconnection, and Fake UPI.

5. Action-Oriented, Not Just Informative

Doesn't just say "Be careful". Generates a 1-click police complaint report and links directly to 1930 and Chakshu.

6. Privacy First

No passwords or bank credentials stored. Content is analyzed in-memory; only anonymized preview hashes are kept.

❓ 5. Anticipated Judge Questions & Winning Answers

"Why not just give the message to ChatGPT with a simple prompt?"
Answer: "Three reasons: Speed, Reliability, and Cost. ChatGPT takes 3-5 seconds and can hallucinate fake reasons. We do it in 250ms. More importantly, if an LLM is asked about a brand new scam domain, it has no web scanner or domain entropy rules. Our system combines deterministic brand lookalike algorithms with LLMs, and works even when the AI API is offline."
"What if scammers use innocent wording with a brand-new phishing domain?"
Answer: "Our URL inspection pipeline decomposes the domain into TLD, subdomains, Shannon entropy, and Levenshtein distance against 40+ trusted brands (SBI, HDFC, FedEx). Even if the text is polite, an anomalous domain like sbi-netbanking.xyz triggers our critical rule override, flooring the score at Dangerous immediately."
"Is it safe to analyze a malicious website? Won't your server get compromised?"
Answer: "Yes, completely safe. We use a sandboxed HTTP client that never executes JavaScript, caps downloads at 1 MB, blocks more than 5 redirects, and sets a strict 3-second timeout. We inspect raw static HTML only."
"How do you prevent false alarms on innocent urgent emails?"
Answer: "We eliminated single-word triggers. A word like 'tonight' or 'urgent' alone will never flag danger. A scam verdict requires a dangerous combination — such as urgency PLUS credential requests, or threats PLUS unverified phone numbers."
